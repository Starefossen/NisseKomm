Locked-off surveillance-camera feed. Absolutely static camera, identical framing and lighting, no cuts, no zoom in or out. The last frame must be identical to the first frame. No audio. Nobody in the room. Snow falls outside the window, the candles flicker gently. The only real motion: the small blue folded paper bird with frost on it, at its small size (about as long as the microphone, never larger), starts sitting on the left of the windowsill. After a moment it opens its paper wings, lifts off and glides slowly and smoothly, like a paper glider, in one calm low arc over the desk, skims just above the papers on the desk so one loose sheet lifts slightly and settles flat again, then glides back the same calm way and lands on the same spot on the windowsill, folds its wings and sits still exactly as at the start for the last seconds. The bird stays solid and visible the whole time: it never fades, never vanishes, never appears from nowhere, never changes size or colour. The microphone lies flat on its side on the desk and never moves, the switchboard stays still, no animal moves or grows: the tiny mouse on the shelf stays exactly where it is and only twitches its whiskers. No people, no hands, no smoke, no steam. Nothing else moves.
