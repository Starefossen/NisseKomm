Locked-off high surveillance-camera view of the village. Absolutely static camera, identical framing, lighting and buildings, no cuts, no zoom. The last frame must be identical to the first frame. No audio. No people are visible in the first two seconds or the last two seconds. Every door, the gate doors and every window stay completely still and closed the whole time; no door ever opens. Buildings stay completely still. A heavy snowstorm: thick snow blows from left to right across the whole village in strong gusts. The wind blows one way only, left to right: the chimney smoke is torn sideways to the right, in the same direction as the snow, never against it. Window lights flicker softly. In the middle of the clip one or two tiny villagers, hunched and huddled against the wind, walk very slowly along the edge of the square from one side street to another and leave the frame well before the end; they barely move, no running, no waving. They never fade in or out. People are tiny seen from this height: a person is no taller than the counter of a small market stall, about half the height of the red mailbox, far smaller than a house door.
