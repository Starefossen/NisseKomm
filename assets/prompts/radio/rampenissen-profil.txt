Scene: A small, mischievous Christmas elf (nisse) sends a short radio message to three children in Bergen from the floor of their living room, early in the morning. He is excited, a little out of breath, and proud of his pranks.

DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Voice: Rampenissen. A sweet, silly, high, light voice; cheerful and breathless, with short giggles. Keep the pitch high from the first word to the last.
Pace: quick and bouncy, about two and a half words per second; short pauses at full stops, no long pauses.
Accent: native Northern Norwegian, Bodø and Salten (nordlandsk), all the way through. Never Eastern Norwegian, never Bergen, never English.
Dialect rules:
- Lilting, sing-song melody that rises and falls inside each phrase.
- Tapped, rolled front R (as in «ikkje», «dokker», «prøve»), never a guttural Bergen R.
- «æ» (I) is said like the vowel in «fæl»; «mæ», «dæ», «sæ» the same.
- «ikkje» with a soft kj sound, never «ikke»; «dokker» (you all), «dem» (they), «ka» (what), «kor» (where), «koffer» (why), «kjem» (comes), «e» (is), «va» (was).
- Short vowels stay short: «takk», «sjø», «heim», «meir».
- Read the words exactly as spelled; the spelling is already nordlandsk.
