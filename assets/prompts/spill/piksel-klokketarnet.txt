Scene: the top room of the golden clock tower on the square. A small room of warm golden timber. Centre: a big open arch looking out over the snow-covered roofs and lanterns of the village far below under a deep blue starry polar night with light snowfall drifting in. Top: three brass bells of different sizes hanging from the beams. Left wall: the clockwork, large and small gears, a weight on a chain and a small brass dial with hands. Right: the back of the large glass clock face, glowing warm from behind, hands visible as dark bars. Lower left: a steep ladder going down through a hatch in the floor. One lantern on a hook. Rime on the beams.
