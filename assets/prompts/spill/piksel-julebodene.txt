Scene: the two market stalls on the square, seen close up at eye level. Two small timber stalls side by side with snow on their roofs and lanterns hanging from the eaves. Baskets of red and blue frozen berries on the shelves of both stalls. The right stall has a hand-painted blank wooden sign nailed to its post. Also for sale: a crate of clementines, a basket of knitted mittens, and a small steaming pot on a wooden table in front. In each stall stands a stall-keeper elf wrapped in wool shawl, cape and knitted hat, one short and round, one tall and thin, both looking at the viewer. Behind them the clock tower and houses of the square, soft in the falling snow. Golden lantern light against the dark blue night.
