Scene: inside Winter's parcel hall behind the mailbox on the square. A long timber hall. A wooden conveyor belt with brass rollers runs diagonally from the lower left into the room, carrying parcels in brown paper with string. Centre: a tall brass sorting machine with funnels, wheels, levers and small hatches, polished and tidy. Left wall: high shelves with neat stacks of forms and heavy ledgers, every pile aligned. Back right: Winter's raised office desk with a green banker's lamp, a row of rubber stamps, an ink bottle and a perfectly straight pile of paper; a small window to the square beside it. Nobody in the room. Green lamp glow over the desk, gold from the brass, cold blue from the window. Everything in rows, almost silent.
